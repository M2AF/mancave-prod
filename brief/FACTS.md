## ChainLens

- What it is: search the web and explore onchain activity in one place.
- Website: https://www.chainlensnft.info
- A browser-based portfolio scanner for public addresses across 24 networks, including 18 EVM networks plus Solana, Polkadot, Tron and Cardano.
- Search: search the web, find App Hub entries, and recognise wallet addresses to scan. Search is the landing page.
- Scanner: connect a wallet or enter public addresses to see NFTs, tokens, balances and transaction activity.
- Market: top coins and price charts, plus a New Listings view of exchange listing announcements.
- Magic Swap: find tokens and request quotes. The connected wallet does the signing.
- Profile: sign in with a supported wallet, Google, Discord or a passkey.
- Messenger: World Chat and friend direct messages, for eligible signed-in accounts.
- Themes: colour themes and art themes.
- Runs on: the web.

## Magic Money

- Full name: Magic Money Wallet.
- What it is: a self-custody, multi-chain crypto wallet, by ChainLens.
- Source and downloads: https://github.com/M2AF/Magic-Money-Wallet (latest release: https://github.com/M2AF/Magic-Money-Wallet/releases/latest)
- Available as: a desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux), a Chrome extension, and Android and iOS apps.
- One seed phrase covers 24 default mainnet networks. You can add other EVM networks.
- Ecosystems: EVM, Solana, Cardano, Bitcoin, Polkadot, Tron, Dogecoin, Monero, Zcash, Midnight.
- Self-custody: the recovery phrase is encrypted at rest, and the interface never sees private keys.
- Swaps: same-chain and cross-chain swaps built in.
- dApp browser: connects to dApps, with Magic Guard ad blocking and a Tor Mode.
- Privacy Mode and Testnet Mode, multi-account support, and art themes.
- Unlock with Windows Hello, Touch ID or Android biometrics.

Accuracy rules for this product:
- Network support covers what the portfolio can show. Swap and send support varies by chain and route, so never say every feature works on every network.
- Don't call it audited, unhackable, risk-free or guaranteed. It's a self-custody wallet, so the user is responsible for their recovery phrase.
- Don't give financial advice or promise returns.

## AfterGlow

- Full name: Afterglow Studio. Use "AfterGlow" as the brand name, as in the logo.
- What it is: create, curate, generate, publish and manage NFT collections, in a local or private hosted workspace.
- Layer-based generation: import folders of trait layers, with rarity weights set per trait.
- Canvas editing: multi-layer selection, moving, scaling, rotating, cropping and drawing.
- Trait rules: linked front and back artwork, exclusions and incompatibilities are handled during generation.
- Your files stay safe: it works on a copy of your artwork, not the original folder.
- It ships with no collection artwork, wallets or credentials.
- Related: AfterMarket, a trait market. Mention it only by name, with no details.
- Public link: none has been provided, so don't show a website or download button.
- Runs on: a local or private hosted install.

## ChainLens Arcade

- Brand name: ChainLens Arcade. This is the new name. Use it everywhere, and don't call the arcade "DUO Arcade" on this site.
- What it is: free multiplayer games in your browser. Play with friends or bots.
- Play: https://duo.magicmoney.dev
- Note: that site still shows the old "DUO Arcade" name for now.
- Six games. These game names stay as they are:
  - DUO: a free card game. Team up and share one hand, or go SOLO with classic rules. DUO teams run 2v2, 3v3, 4v4 and 2v2v2v2; SOLO takes 2 to 10 players.
  - Poker: Texas Hold'em and Blackjack with friends and bots, using free play chips.
  - Pool: 3D 8-ball with friends and bots. Solo head to head, or Duo in teams of two.
  - Swarm: pick a hero and survive a growing horde. Co-op for 2 to 4 players.
  - Clash: a platform fighter. Rack up damage and launch your rivals off the stage. Free for all or 2v2.
  - World: an open-world adventure. Explore, fight monsters, loot, trade and quest with friends.
- Guests can play without an account.
