ChainLens

Search the web and explore onchain activity in one place.

ChainLens is a browser-based tool that brings web search and onchain activity together.

Open ChainLens, search, and move from the web to a wallet's activity without switching tools.

Open ChainLens

Features

Search

Search the web, find App Hub entries, and have wallet addresses recognised so you can scan them. Search is the landing page.

Portfolio scanner

Connect a wallet or enter public addresses to see NFTs, tokens, balances and transaction activity. The scanner covers 24 networks, including 18 EVM networks plus Solana, Polkadot, Tron and Cardano.

Market

See top coins and price charts, plus a New Listings view of exchange listing announcements.

Magic Swap

Find tokens and request quotes. The connected wallet does the signing.

Profile

Sign in with a supported wallet, Google, Discord or a passkey.

Messenger

World Chat and friend direct messages, available to eligible signed-in accounts.

Themes

Choose from colour themes and art themes.

Magic Money Wallet

One self-custody wallet for many chains.

Magic Money Wallet is a self-custody, multi-chain crypto wallet, made by ChainLens.

It's a self-custody wallet, so you are responsible for your recovery phrase. The recovery phrase is encrypted at rest, and the interface never sees private keys.

Download latest release View on GitHub

Features

Many networks, one seed phrase

One seed phrase covers 24 default mainnet networks, and you can add other EVM networks. Ecosystems include EVM, Solana, Cardano, Bitcoin, Polkadot, Tron, Dogecoin, Monero, Zcash and Midnight. Network support covers what the portfolio can show; swap and send support varies by chain and route.

Self-custody by design

The recovery phrase is encrypted at rest, and the interface never sees private keys.

Built-in swaps

Same-chain and cross-chain swaps are built in. Availability varies by chain and route.

dApp browser

Connect to dApps, with Magic Guard ad blocking and a Tor Mode.

Privacy Mode and Testnet Mode

Switch on Privacy Mode, or use Testnet Mode.

Multi-account support and art themes

Use more than one account, and choose an art theme.

Biometric unlock

Unlock with Windows Hello, Touch ID or Android biometrics.

Available as

A desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux), a Chrome extension, and Android and iOS apps.

Important to know

  • You are responsible for your recovery phrase. Keep it safe and private.
  • Not every feature works on every network.
  • Nothing on this site is financial advice, and no returns are promised.

AfterGlow

Build and manage NFT collections in your own workspace.

AfterGlow is for creating, curating, generating, publishing and managing NFT collections.

It runs as a local or private hosted install.

Features

Layer-based generation

Import folders of trait layers and set rarity weights per trait.

Canvas editing

Select multiple layers, then move, scale, rotate, crop and draw.

Trait rules

Linked front and back artwork is supported, and exclusions and incompatibilities are handled during generation.

Your files stay safe

AfterGlow works on a copy of your artwork, not the original folder.

Nothing bundled

It ships with no collection artwork, wallets or credentials.

ChainLens Arcade

Free multiplayer games in your browser.

ChainLens Arcade has six games you can play with friends or bots, right in your browser. Guests can play without an account.

Play ChainLens Arcade

ChainLens Arcade is the new name. The game site still shows its old name, "DUO Arcade", while the rename finishes.

The games

DUO

A free card game. Team up and share one hand, or go SOLO with classic rules. DUO teams run 2v2, 3v3, 4v4 and 2v2v2v2; SOLO takes 2 to 10 players.

Poker

Texas Hold'em and Blackjack with friends and bots, using free play chips.

Pool

3D 8-ball with friends and bots. Play solo head to head, or Duo in teams of two.

Swarm

Pick a hero and survive a growing horde. Co-op for 2 to 4 players.

Clash

A platform fighter. Rack up damage and launch your rivals off the stage. Free for all or 2v2.

World

An open-world adventure. Explore, fight monsters, loot, trade and quest with friends.
